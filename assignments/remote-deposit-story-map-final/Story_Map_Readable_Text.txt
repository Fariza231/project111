Biometric success paths
Fingerprint: after bank-verified sign-in and explicit opt-in, a supported phone accepts a recognized fingerprint and opens the
authorized session. Touch ID: after the same enrollment/consent, a supported iPhone accepts Touch ID and opens the
authorized session. Both methods retain a non-biometric fallback and follow bank session/lockout rules; the bank does not
receive a raw fingerprint image.
Scope check
Required functions: downloads for iPhone, Android and Windows Phone; portal or alternative sign-in; balances; front AND
back capture; amount/details; destination account; image-versus-details validation; secure e-check deposit; bank offers.
Fingerprint and Touch ID are additional authentication options, not replacements for basic bank verification.

Acceptance examples
• A valid deposit: sign in, view balances, choose an eligible account, capture front/back, enter amount, pass image/detail
checks, review and submit once; receive a bank reference and status.
• A failed login: show a safe error, grant no account access, and offer retry or bank recovery. Expired alternative codes are
rejected; biometric failures fall back to an approved method.
• A camera/image error: a missing permission, blurred image or absent check side blocks the affected step and gives a clear
retry/retake route.
• A data mismatch: invalid amount or image-versus-entered-detail mismatch blocks submission until the customer corrects
it.
• A submission timeout: check the existing request status before resending; an unknown result is not displayed as a
successful cleared deposit.
• Banking without promotions: an unavailable offer never prevents viewing a balance or submitting a check.
Explicit assumptions and scope
The bank provides existing identity, remote-capture enrollment/eligibility, balances, check-validation and
deposit-processing/status services. Alternative authentication may use a bank-approved verified code; fingerprint and Touch
ID are optional, supported-device conveniences following bank authentication, with a non-biometric fallback.
Check images use protected temporary storage/transport and bank-side duplicate/tamper checks; “fraud-proof” in the vision
is treated as a fraud-resistance requirement rather than an absolute guarantee. Bank acknowledgement, clearing and funds
availability are distinct; the app displays bank-provided statuses and balances.
All three platforms in the fictional case remain explicit, including Windows Phone; real-world platform/store feasibility must
be agreed before implementation. Desktop scanners, browser plug-ins, scanner purchases, portal account administration
and bank-side clearing are outside this mobile map. Help links may use the existing bank support service; building that
service is not mobile scope.

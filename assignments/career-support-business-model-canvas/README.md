# Affordable Career Support for University Students

Project title: **Affordable Career Support for University Students**

Upload [Career_Support_Business_Model_Canvas.pdf](Career_Support_Business_Model_Canvas.pdf) to Coursera. It is one landscape page containing all nine Business Model Canvas building blocks in the standard layout. Click the PDF and use **Download raw file** to save it.

[PNG version / preview](Career_Support_Business_Model_Canvas.png)

The canvas consolidates the earlier challenge statement, customer segment, value propositions, channels, relationships, revenue streams, resources, activities, partnerships and cost structure. Final-year students are the primary users/buyers; universities can also fund cohort access. Reusable content, defined feedback allowances and separately priced intensive coaching connect the affordability promise to adviser-capacity costs. Pricing and demand are proposed assumptions to validate through a pilot; no unverified market results or profit claims are presented.

The PDF was checked for one-page output, the presence of all nine headings, readable layout and unclipped text. No personal author name or submission date is included. Editable content is in Canvas_Content.json; source/create_canvas.py regenerates the PDF and PNG using ReportLab and PyMuPDF.
